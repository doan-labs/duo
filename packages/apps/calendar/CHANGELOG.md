# 1.1.0

The tablet layout: sidebar with calendars and a mini month, Day, Week, Month and
Year views, events that are created, edited, deleted and searched, and stored in
app storage.

Month draws the sheet each display asks for. Inside, it is the Mac's: ruled both
ways in a hairline well under the UIKit separator, the weekend shaded, numbers
hung on the right of their day, the picked day lifted whole. On the cover it is
the phone's: no rules, no shading, the number centred over its dots. Events pack
into lanes either way, so a multi-day event is a single bar across the days it
covers, and a week only draws the rows the month has instead of ruling an empty
sixth. A day that runs out of lanes counts the rest and opens on Day.

Day and Week are ruled to match, with the weekend shaded, the hours hung on
their own lines, and now drawn the way Calendar draws it: pale across the week,
solid with a dot on today, and the time itself in the gutter. The sidebar lists
calendars as tinted checkboxes and its mini month follows the pane instead of
stranding September under a February sheet. Paging slides the sheet the way the
arrow points; arriving at a view raises it.

A fresh install opens on a working month around today, the keynote the
home-screen widget shows included.
