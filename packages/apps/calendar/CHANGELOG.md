# 1.1.0

The tablet layout: sidebar with calendars and a mini month, Day, Week, Month and
Year views, events that are created, edited, deleted and searched, and stored in
app storage.

Month is a sheet ruled week by week rather than a spreadsheet: one hairline per
week, no column rules and no weekend wash, day numbers on the footnote step
centred over the column, and events packed into lanes so a multi-day event is a
single bar across the days it covers. A day that runs out of lanes counts the
rest and opens on Day. The sidebar lists calendars the way iOS does, a filled
dot and a checkmark, hollow when the calendar is hidden. A fresh install opens
on a working month around today, the keynote the home-screen widget shows
included.
