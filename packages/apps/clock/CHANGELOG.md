# 1.1.0

The Clock app rebuilt end to end as the iPadOS Clock: a sidebar on the wide
display, a floating tab bar on the cover, and four working tabs. World Clock
lists cities with relative-day offsets and an add-city sheet with search.
Alarms add, edit, toggle and delete, with an iOS wheel picker for the time,
repeat days, sounds, snooze, and a full-screen snooze/stop alert that only the
owning display sounds. Stopwatch runs a digital and an analog face off one
shared timer with laps, best and worst highlights. Timers pick hours, minutes
and seconds on wheels, run as orange-ring cards that pause, resume and cancel,
and remember recents. All state lives in app storage, so the two display
copies agree while only the owner fires sounds.
