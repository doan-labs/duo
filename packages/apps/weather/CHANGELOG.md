# 1.0.0

Isolated Weather with owner-only forecast requests, shared persistent preferences,
refresh commands, delegated geolocation and declarative widget snapshots.

Redesigned after the iOS Weather app: large hero temperature, condition-driven sky
backgrounds with moon, stars, clouds, rain, snow and fog layers, frosted glass cards,
SF Symbol condition glyphs, an hourly strip with sunrise and sunset, ten-day rows with
temperature range bars and a today marker, visual detail tiles (UV, sunrise/sunset arc,
wind compass, feels like, 24-hour precipitation bars, humidity, visibility, pressure
gauge, air quality from Open-Meteo), sky-card locations list, and a page-dot toolbar
with a bottom bar on the outer display.
