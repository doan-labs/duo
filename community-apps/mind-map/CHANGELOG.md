# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Infinite canvas with pan, zoom, fit and drag-to-move nodes.
- Add children, rename and recolour nodes, delete branches with a two-tap
  confirm.
- Multiple maps in an on-device library; switch, create and delete maps from
  the panel or the cover sheet.
- The open map and selection follow the fold through `os.session`; the library
  persists through `os.storage` with validation and repair on load.
