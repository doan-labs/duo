# Recipe Box

Recipe Box keeps your recipes one tap from the stove. Save a dish with its
ingredients and ordered steps, tick off ingredients as you prep, and cook in a
step-by-step mode that remembers where you stopped. Recipes and progress live
in the app's private storage, while the open screen mirrors through the
session, so folding the phone hands the same recipe - even a half-written edit -
to the other display.

The cover view keeps the whole flow within reach: the list, the recipe, the
checklist and every step. Unfolded, ingredients sit beside the instructions and
the cooking step card gets room to breathe.

Two sample recipes ship in the box; delete them once your own are in.
