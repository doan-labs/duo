# Flappy Duo

Flappy Duo is Flappy Bird, except the bird is the Duo logo and the game has opinions about you. Tap, click or press space to fold. Fly the D through the hinge pillars. Do not hit anything. Simple, right?

Every score you reach earns commentary. Every death earns a roast and a medal you did not deserve. Your best score is saved, so the game can remind you that you used to be better.

The canvas fills whichever display is active, so it plays on the cover as well as the inner display.
