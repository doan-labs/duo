# Flappy Duo

Flappy Bird, except the bird is the iPhone Duo and the game has opinions about you. Tap, click or press space to fold. The two halves flap like wings, the hinge glows, and every fold is deducted from a 200,000-fold hinge rating you will never reach.

Fly between glass slabs labelled with everything that ever went wrong with a phone launch. Every score earns commentary. Every death is a Hinge Failure alert with a roast, a medal you did not earn, and a button to buy another Duo for $2,399. The game keeps a running total of what you have spent.

The game gets worse the better you do. At three points the slabs start drifting. At six the beta blur kicks in. At nine fake notifications drop over the action. At thirteen it speeds up. Each one is announced like a software update you did not ask for.

Buying another Duo opens a Duo Pay sheet. Double-press the side button on the right edge of the screen to confirm, and a notification tells you that you have been charged. A home-screen widget shows your best score, what you have spent and how much of the hinge is gone.

Every fifth slab has a cartoon executive peeking out of it with something to say when you fly past. Likenesses are drawn as a few circles and a pair of glasses, for fun only.

Best score, lifetime folds and money spent persist across launches. Tiny synthesised flap, score and crash sounds are generated in the browser with no assets. The canvas fills whichever display is active, so it plays on the cover as well as the inner display.
