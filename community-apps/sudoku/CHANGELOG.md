# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- First release: deterministic daily puzzle and easy/medium/hard free play.
- Generator digs symmetric clue pairs only while the puzzle keeps a unique
  solution, and grades each puzzle by the solving techniques required
  (singles, pairs and locked candidates, then search) so the difficulty label
  is honest.
- Number pad with remaining counts, pencil marks, erase, undo, conflicts in
  red, a hint that explains its deduction, restart and new game.
- Daily and free-play boards persist and resume across relaunches; the live
  game - selection, notes and clock included - follows the fold between
  displays.
- Wide layout puts the board beside the pad, tools and stats; the cover keeps
  a readable full board with touch controls. Light and dark themes.
