# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- 41 travel phrases in five groups (Basics, Getting around, Eating out, Shopping, Help)
  in Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese and Vietnamese. Japanese
  carries a romanisation under every phrase.
- Show view: the phrase set as large as its longest word allows, with the English meaning
  below, so it can be held up to the person you are speaking to.
- Cover-first layout: the list and the show view are two screens on the cover and sit
  side by side on the inner display. A phrase opened on one display is the one the
  other display shows after the fold.
- Search across English, the chosen language and its romanisation, ignoring accents.
- Starred phrases, the chosen language and the open phrase survive a fold and a relaunch
  (the open phrase lasts for the session).
- Works offline: no network, no permissions, no account.
