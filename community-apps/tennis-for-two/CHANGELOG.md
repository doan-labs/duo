# 1.0.0

First community release. A side-view rally after William Higinbotham's 1958 oscilloscope
game: a phosphor ball under gravity, a court line and a net. Tap where the ball
should land to strike it, play the bot on three levels or a friend on one screen, first to 11.
Both displays draw the same match; only the one you look at plays. No network, no device
permissions.
